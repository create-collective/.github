# Getting help

- **Want to ask other owners?** Join us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/zEg7pwMrm).
- **Using Create Companion?** Start with its
  [install guide](https://github.com/create-collective/create-companion/blob/main/INSTALL.md) and
  README. Ask questions in its
  [Discussions](https://github.com/create-collective/create-companion/discussions), and report bugs
  as [issues](https://github.com/create-collective/create-companion/issues/new/choose).
- **Something wrong with your keyboard?** Check
  [Troubleshooting](https://create-collective.github.io/Create-knowledge-base/troubleshooting/) and
  [Recovery](https://create-collective.github.io/Create-knowledge-base/recovery/) in the knowledge
  base before changing anything.
- **Questions about the hardware or protocol?** The
  [knowledge base](https://create-collective.github.io/Create-knowledge-base/) covers what's known,
  and its [open questions](https://create-collective.github.io/Create-knowledge-base/open-questions/)
  page covers what isn't.
- **Orders, refunds or warranty?** We aren't Naya and can't help with these.
